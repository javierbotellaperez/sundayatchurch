SUNDAY AT CHURCH / PHASE 01

Welcome to the Confessional.

A place to leave a thought, a memory or a confession about sauna culture. You don't need to be a regular. You just need a story.

Throughout history, the Church has often approached desire and sexuality through the language of sin and guilt. This project borrows the ritual of confession as a way to question the shame we may have been taught to carry. This is not a religious confession or a place of judgement. It is simply a space to speak freely, in your own words.

Begin with the opening confession. The ten core prompts form a shared thread; optional questions are there if you would like to say more. Skip anything you do not want to answer.

A CONFESSION, WITHOUT JUDGEMENT

Here, confession is not an admission of wrongdoing. We borrow its language to make room for the things we have been taught to keep quiet: desire, pleasure, shame, tenderness, memory and everything in between. There is no penance to perform and no right answer to give. Speak only about what you want to share.
